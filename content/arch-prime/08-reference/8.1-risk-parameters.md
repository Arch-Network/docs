This page is **Arch Prime's** V1 risk parameter set in one table, with each value's status
and the owner who can change it. These are the brokerage's parameters — Arch Prime sets
them and Arch Prime can change them. They are not constants of Arch Network, and nothing
in this table is a property of the chain. Where a parameter has no agreed value, it is
listed with no value.

## The formulas these parameters feed

```
collateralValue = Σ (quantity × price × (1 − haircut))
healthFactor    = collateralValue / totalDebt
liquidation triggered when healthFactor < 1

supportable user collateral = (committed depth × utilisation) ÷ (leverage − 1)
equity-wiping NAV impairment = E / V     (E = account equity, V = deployed position)
```

V1 margin is deliberately additive. There is no correlation modelling, and the capital
efficiency gain comes almost entirely from counting the deployed position rather than from
netting — which is also why risk moves to the deployed leg, why that leg is whitelisted and
underwritten, and why it is liquidated first.

Reference values for reading the leverage ceiling. These are arithmetic, not parameters.

| Leverage | Implied LTV |
|---|---|
| 1.5× | ≈33% |
| 2.0× | 50% |
| 3.0× | ≈67% |
| 4.0× | 75% |

## The contested and undefined figures, named

This section exists so that no reader has to reconcile the corpus themselves, and so that no
one averages the disagreeing numbers into a plausible-looking figure.

> **[OPEN — max LTV of 80% is the 21 August gameplan figure and is contested across the
> corpus (the canonical documentation, a separate internal analysis, and the parameters
> approved for the deployed market each carry a different value); it is stated here only
> with this flag until Risk reconciles · owner: Risk]**

> **[OPEN — the liquidation threshold (LLTV) has no agreed value; the figure carried in
> four corpus files is inconsistent with the deployed market, and every Prime colour band is
> arithmetically derived from it, so all derived bands inherit the error and none may be
> published · owner: Risk]**

> **[OPEN — the per-account borrow cap of 2% of available depth is a recommended starting
> value, not a ratified parameter, and the step-down schedule as depth grows is not set ·
> owner: Risk]**

> **[OPEN — per-asset haircut values are not published; the methodology is defined but the
> inputs and resulting values for each eligible asset are not · owner: Risk]**

> **[OPEN — the length of the liquidation window is not specified, which leaves the rank-1
> unwindability test (a position must be unwindable inside the window, by sale of the receipt
> or by redemption with the strategy) without a measurable bound on either route · owner:
> Risk]**

> **[OPEN — which unwind route is primary (sale of the receipt or redemption with the
> strategy), who selects it at closeout, and whether it varies by strategy, are not settled;
> both routes are carried in this table, neither as the normal path · owner: Product / Risk]**

> **[OPEN — secondary-market depth for a receipt is an eligibility and haircut input with no
> definition: no measure, no observation source or cadence, no sufficiency level, no stated
> behaviour when depth deteriorates, and no decision on whether it is recorded in the
> collateral registry · owner: Risk / Protocol Engineering]**

> **[OPEN — the haircut and liquidation rank that Prime BTC and Prime Dollar carry when used
> as collateral are undecided, alongside their accrual method, mint and redemption process,
> redemption SLA and book segregation · owner: Product]**

> **[OPEN — the take rate / financing spread is undefined across the entire corpus and no
> figure, range or approximation may be published; it is a named launch and diligence
> blocker · owner: CEO — Operating Frame §5]**

> **[OPEN — per-strategy and per-manager concentration caps do not exist in V1 and their
> phase-two values are not set · owner: Risk]**

## What does not hold

- **A parameter is not a bound on loss.** These values determine when closeout begins and in
  what order. They do not determine the price at which it completes.
- **The derived figures move with their inputs.** Max implied leverage is computed from max
  LTV. If max LTV changes, 5.0× changes with it.
- **Absence of a row is not absence of a risk.** Market tail risk, strategy NAV impairment
  and the validator honest-majority assumption are not parameters and cannot be tuned in this
  table.
- **This page is the reference, not the source.** Where a public surface disagrees with this
  table, the surface is stale — correcting them is part of the disclosure gate on the
  capacity ladder.
