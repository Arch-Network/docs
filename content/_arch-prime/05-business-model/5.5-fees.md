This page states what a Prime account will be charged on, and which of those charges have a
number attached today. Most do not.

## What you are charged on

**Financing.** Borrowing against collateral accrues a financing cost for as long as the debt
is outstanding. The rate moves with utilisation of the lending depth, and the rate curve
inflects at 90% utilisation. Inside that rate sits the spread split between the third-party
liquidity providers who fund the borrow and Arch Prime, which does not fund it.

**Swaps.** Whether a Prime account swaps at all, at what point in the account's life it
would, and what it is charged for doing so are not specified in this set.

**The strategy you deploy into.** A deployed position is a position in someone's strategy,
and strategy economics are charged inside it — reducing the net asset value your account
holds rather than appearing as a line item against your account.

**Bitcoin settlement.** Arch Network settles to Bitcoin, and settlement appears there as an
ordinary Taproot-signed transaction, so it carries Bitcoin network fees like any other
transaction.

There is no minimum account size and no minimum ticket, so nothing in the fee structure is
sized to price a small account out.

## What has a number

Nothing. Every charge named above is either undecided or unpublished.

## What does not, and what each one is waiting on

**The financing spread and Arch Prime's take rate.** No figure, no range, and no approximation
exists anywhere in the governing documents. This is not a pricing strategy held back for
commercial reasons; the decision has not been made. It is a named launch blocker and a
diligence blocker, and "take rate defined" is one of the four gates on the capacity ladder.

> **[OPEN — the take rate / financing spread is undefined across the entire corpus; no
> figure, range or approximation may be published or quoted until it is set · owner: CEO —
> named launch and diligence blocker, Operating Frame §5]**

**The strategy fee split.** How strategy economics divide between the manager and Arch Prime
is undecided, alongside the rest of the Prime BTC and Prime Dollar mechanics — accrual method,
mint and redemption process, redemption SLA, whether the strategy book is segregated, and the
haircut and liquidation rank the assets carry as collateral.

> **[OPEN — the fee split on Prime BTC and Prime Dollar is undecided, together with accrual
> method, mint and redemption process, redemption SLA, book segregation, and the haircut and
> liquidation rank these assets carry as collateral · owner: Product]**

**Swap-related charges.** No page in this set explains what a swap is inside a Prime account
or when one occurs, so no swap charge is stated here.

> **[OPEN — swap-related charges in a Prime account are not specified: neither the
> circumstances in which a Prime account swaps nor the fee that applies when it does is
> described anywhere in this set · owner: Product]**

**Closeout economics.** Whether closeout carries a penalty to the account, and how a party
performing a liquidation is compensated, are not specified.

> **[OPEN — closeout economics are unspecified: whether a liquidation penalty is charged to
> the account, and how liquidators are compensated · owner: Risk]**

**Who pays settlement fees.** That Bitcoin settlement costs a network fee is a property of
Bitcoin. Whether that cost is passed to the account, netted, or absorbed is a product
decision that has not been taken.

> **[OPEN — pass-through of Bitcoin network fees on settlement is undecided: charged to the
> account, netted, or absorbed · owner: Product]**

## What does not hold

- **No rate on this page is final.** Any financing rate discussed elsewhere, in any
  conversation, is not a commitment.
- **A missing rate is not a low rate.** Nothing here should be read as a signal about where
  pricing will land.
- **Charges you do not see are still charges.** Strategy economics reduce the net asset value
  of your deployed position rather than billing your account, and NAV is what your equity is
  measured against in a levered position.
