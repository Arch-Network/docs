title: What is live in V1
---


This page states the scope of V1 exactly: what you can do on day one, what you cannot, and
which capabilities are staged for later releases.

## What V1 is

V1 is the full product suite with the listed yield strategies at the centre. At the outset
the main activity is using the margining system and those strategies for yield.

The hook is a marketplace of different types of yield products, with leverage coming from
looping those assets. That is the differentiating feature and it is what the product is
built around.

## V1 is not cross-margin

This is the point most often misread, so it is stated directly.

V1 gives you an experience that **feels like** a cross-margin account. Underneath, it uses
isolated pools. You borrow against collateral, and leverage comes from multi-instruction
looping of those assets on a per-strategy basis. Margin is additive: positions do not
offset each other, correlations are not modelled, and risk is not netted across your book.

True cross-margining — one netted risk view across heterogeneous collateral — is V2. If
you are sizing a position on the assumption that a hedge in one leg reduces the margin
requirement in another, that assumption does not hold in V1.

The capital efficiency gain in V1 comes almost entirely from counting the deployed
position, not from netting. Risk moves to the deployed leg, which is levered, which is why
those positions are whitelisted, underwritten, and liquidated first.

## In scope / out of scope

| Capability | V1 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Post native Bitcoin as collateral | **In scope** | Stays a UTXO on Bitcoin under Taproot spending conditions |
| Margin account with a single health factor | **In scope** | Owner, positions, debts, one health factor |
| Borrow against posted collateral | **In scope** | Isolated pools, per-account borrow cap |
| Leverage by looping the deployed asset | **In scope** | Multi-instruction looping, per-strategy |
| Marketplace of yield products | **In scope** | The differentiating feature of V1 |
| Rule-based liquidation, Bitcoin last | **In scope** | Deployed positions rank 1, Bitcoin rank 99 |
| Trading | **Out of scope** | What you are trading is yield |
| Cross-margining / netted portfolio risk | **Out of scope** | V2 |
| Heterogeneous collateral with netted risk | **Out of scope** | V2 |
| Correlation netting | **Out of scope** | Margin is additive in V1 |
| Cross-account netting | **Out of scope** | — |
| External venue connectivity | **Out of scope** | V2 adds venue integrations |
| "Trade anywhere" | **Out of scope** | Staged; not shipping |
| Tokenized equities, securities-like collateral | **Out of scope** | — |
| Rehypothecation of any kind | **Out of scope** | Permanently, unless explicitly reintroduced |

## What arrives in V2

V2 adds true portfolio margin, heterogeneous collateral, netted risk, and venue
integrations. Nothing on that list should be assumed present in V1 in a partial or
preview form. It is not there.

## Limits on this page

- **Concentration.** V1 runs one whitelisted strategy, which means 100% concentration by
  construction. That is acceptable only because the strategy is hand-underwritten, and it
  is not a precedent. Per-strategy and per-manager caps arrive in phase two.
- **Capacity.** What is technically live and what is available to you are different
  questions. Access is gated by committed lending depth and by a per-account borrow cap,
  not by code readiness.
- **The strategy product itself.** The generic listing requirements are described in
  [Deploying: the yield marketplace](../02-how-it-works/2.4-deploying-the-yield-marketplace).
  The specific product truth for the V1 strategy is not settled:
