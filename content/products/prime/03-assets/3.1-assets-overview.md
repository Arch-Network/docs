This page tells you which assets exist in Arch Prime, what job each one does, and — for the two that are still being specified — exactly which mechanics have been decided and which have not.

## Three assets, three different jobs

Arch Prime uses three assets. They are not variations on each other, and the differences matter more than the shared prefix suggests.

One thing this page cannot tell you, and says so up front: which entity issues each asset and stands behind it. Arch Network holds and settles Bitcoin collateral; Arch Prime extends credit and lists strategies. Neither of those descriptions covers issuing a token, holding a stablecoin reserve at a custodian, or taking a share of a strategy's return, and no governing document assigns those functions to either entity.

> **[OPEN — no governing document states which entity issues archUSD, Prime BTC or Prime Dollar, holds the reserve or strategy book standing behind them, or takes the issuer's side of the fee split. It is not established whether the responsible party is Arch Network, Arch Prime, or a separate entity, so no page in this set can name it and the word "Arch" in the segregation and fee-split items below is unresolved · owner: Product / Finance / Counsel]**

**archUSD** is a 1:1 stablecoin-backed dollar with permissionless mint, custodied at BitGo. It is not a CDP. It wraps dollars that already arrived; it does not manufacture lending capacity. Holding archUSD is holding a claim on dollars someone else already deposited. It is plumbing, not a position.

> **[OPEN — what role archUSD plays in the V1 borrow, settlement and deployment path is not stated in any governing document · owner: Product]**

**Prime BTC** is a derivative asset that gives spot exposure to Bitcoin and also carries yield.

**Prime Dollar** is a derivative asset that gives spot exposure to the dollar and also carries yield. "Prime Dollar" is a working title and the name may change.

The yield on both Prime BTC and Prime Dollar comes from market-neutral, HFT quant-driven strategies managed by Velox Trading. Market-neutral does not mean risk-free. The strategy carries execution, model, counterparty, venue and operational risk, and its yield can go to zero or negative.

So the line that separates the three is not Bitcoin-versus-dollar. It is this: archUSD wraps value that already exists, while Prime BTC and Prime Dollar are positions in a trading strategy whose value can fall.

## Why the third row and the sixth row are the ones to read

Two rows carry most of the decision.

**Where yield comes from** tells you whether the asset can lose value on its own, independent of the market it tracks. archUSD cannot, short of a reserve failure. Prime BTC and Prime Dollar can, because a strategy that is not taking a directional view can still be wrong about spreads, venues, or its own execution.

**How you get out** tells you what happens to the asset under stress — but redemption is only one of the two ways out. A haircut is a function of price volatility, enforcement certainty, and the unwind route, and the unwind route is the input most often forgotten. It has two forms: redemption with the issuer, which is slow but contractual, and sale on the secondary market, which is fast but conditional and withdraws under stress. An asset that can be turned back into repayment inside the liquidation window by neither route cannot be liquidated first, whatever its price chart looks like.

That is why the redemption SLA flag feeds the liquidation rank flag rather than sitting beside it — and why it does not settle it on its own. Executable depth in the instrument is the other input, and no page in this set states any.

## What does not hold

- **These three are not interchangeable dollars.** archUSD and Prime Dollar are both dollar-denominated and they are not the same instrument. One wraps dollars that exist and pays nothing. The other is a position in a strategy that pays yield and can lose value. Confusing them is the single most likely reader error on these pages, and 3.4 is written specifically to prevent it.
- **This page states no yield figure, no haircut, and no liquidation rank for Prime BTC or Prime Dollar.** Those are not omitted for brevity. They have not been decided.
- **A comparison table is not an eligibility list.** Appearing here does not mean an asset is accepted as collateral in V1. V1 collateral is native Bitcoin; heterogeneous collateral arrives with V2.
- **archUSD's 1:1 backing describes what stands behind the unit, not its market price.** What you can execute at a given moment is a liquidity question, not a reserve question. See 3.2.
